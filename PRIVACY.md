# Pixel Trivia Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 19, 2026
**App:** Pixel Trivia, a learn-then-quiz game for the Anna AI OS
**Developer:** Ammar Malik — contact: ammarmal33k@gmail.com

Pixel Trivia is a trivia game in which Bit, a pixel owl, teaches short lessons and then quizzes you on them. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app stores, what it sends to AI, and what control you have. In short: your progress lives in your own Anna account, Pixel Trivia has no servers of its own, and nothing is used for advertising.

## 1. What Pixel Trivia stores

When you use Pixel Trivia inside Anna, the app saves the following to **your Anna account**, using Anna's app storage:

- **Your profile:** the player name and avatar you chose, your favourite subjects, your sound and read-aloud settings, and a random player ID (a string of letters and numbers, used to tell rivals apart in challenges; it is not linked to your Anna account details).
- **Your progress:** XP and level, stars and lesson history for each subject, your daily streak, badges, statistics (rounds, accuracy, best combo), your ten best scores, the titles of lesson cards you've read, and a short list of recent questions so they aren't repeated.
- **Your review pile:** questions you missed (question, options, answer and explanation) with the date each is next due, up to 150.
- **Challenges:** the challenge codes you sent (up to 30), the challenges you played (up to 30) with both players' scores, and your rivals board: each rival's player name, avatar, player ID and your win/loss/draw record against them.

This data is kept so your progress is there on every device where you sign in to Anna. It stays until you delete it (see section 6).

If you open Pixel Trivia outside Anna (for example, a developer preview in a plain web browser), the same information is kept only in that browser's local storage on that device, and no AI is used.

## 2. What is sent to AI, and when

Bit uses **Anna's built-in AI**, either through the app's bundled helper tool or directly. Something is sent only when you take one of these actions:

| When you… | What is sent |
| --- | --- |
| Start a lesson | The subject, topic and difficulty level, and the titles of cards you've already read on that topic |
| Play a lesson quiz or quick quiz | The subject, topic, difficulty, the lesson text (for lesson quizzes), and up to 30 of your recent questions in that subject so Bit doesn't repeat them. The questions Bit writes are then sent back for a second, blind fact-check |
| Tap **WHY? ASK BIT** | That question, its answer and the option you picked |
| Finish a round | The name you chose, the subject and topic, your score, and the questions you got right and missed, for Bit's short recap |

Your review pile, rivals and challenge history are never sent to AI as a whole. The placement round and review rounds use questions already saved in the app or your account, and send nothing unless you tap **WHY? ASK BIT** on one of them.

Anna runs these AI requests on its platform, and Anna may use third-party model providers to do so. If you use your own model key with Anna ("bring your own key"), requests may go to that key's provider. How Anna and those providers handle AI requests is covered by **Anna's own privacy policy and terms** (available on anna.partners). Pixel Trivia does not ask for your data to be used to train AI models.

## 3. Challenges with friends

When you tap **CHALLENGE A FRIEND**, the app creates a message containing a code. The code holds your player name, avatar, player ID, the round's questions and answers, and your score. When you reply to a challenge, the reply code holds your player name, avatar, player ID and score.

These codes are only shared when **you** send them, through your device's share sheet or by copying and pasting them into a chat app of your choice. Anyone who receives the message can read what's in it. Pixel Trivia doesn't send challenges anywhere itself, and the chat app you use has its own privacy policy.

## 4. What the developer can see

Pixel Trivia has no servers, database or analytics of its own. The app's code runs inside Anna, and your data goes only to Anna (section 1) and Anna's AI (section 2).

As the developer, I do not receive copies of your profile, progress, answers or challenges. Anna may show developers aggregate usage statistics for their apps (for example, how many people used the app in a month). Those statistics come from Anna and don't include your game data.

## 5. Read aloud and sound

**Read lessons aloud** uses your browser's or device's built-in speech feature. Sound effects are generated on your device. Neither sends anything to the developer.

## 6. Your choices and control

- **See your data:** everything Pixel Trivia stores is visible in the app (Profile, Scores, Challenges, Review).
- **Report a question:** tap **REPORT** on any question that looks wrong; it is removed from your review pile and added to the questions Bit is told to avoid.
- **Delete everything:** Profile → **RESET ALL PROGRESS** deletes your profile, progress, review pile and challenge history from your Anna account. Challenge messages you already sent stay wherever you sent them.
- **Stop AI processing:** don't start lessons or quick quizzes, and don't tap **WHY? ASK BIT**. Review rounds then work without AI.

Removing the app from Anna may not by itself delete its stored data, so use **RESET ALL PROGRESS** first if you want it gone. For your Anna account itself, see Anna's policies.

## 7. Security

Your data is protected by your Anna account and Anna's platform security. Scores inside challenge codes can't be verified, so treat friends' scores as a friendly game, not a record.

## 8. Cookies, tracking and advertising

Pixel Trivia uses no cookies, ads, trackers or third-party analytics. Its font and graphics are bundled with the app, so opening it doesn't contact other websites. Pixel Trivia never sells or shares your data.

## 9. Children

Pixel Trivia is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from them.
